# THE PLATE ON THE DOOR
## Hawaiʻi campaign spine — Emergency Republic

Use with [the setting bible](hawaii-setting-bible.md), the core moves and WHAT YOU OWE. All firms, staff, contracts and anomalous incidents below are game fiction.

## The firm

**Halloran & Vey — Marine Casualty & Loss Adjustment** is a fictional small practice in downtown Honolulu. The rented office, brass plate and files give the crew a reason to investigate cargo discrepancies, missing workers and disputed findings. Do not invent a Hawaiian law requiring two principals or assert that a report automatically becomes legal truth.

Otty (Ottoline Sabban) maintains the files and continuity. Mrs. Ansel is a fictional contract liaison, not a real official or purported real licensing office. Access comes from the specific client's permissions, and can be questioned or revoked; neither person grants unrestricted access to real facilities. The unresolved Vey name is a character hook, not proof of a cosmic explanation.

## The emergency

Start from the chosen scenario date. September 2026 is the beginning of the alternate timeline, not a claim that the later Observer Trials already exist. Track the September Compression, October Emergency Logistics Order, December prediction-linked arrest, spring 2027 Branch Event One, and late 2028–2029 Observer Trials in order. The setting bible distinguishes accepted history from still-open future developments.

The crew's work product is a finding. A client may want certainty the evidence cannot support. A warning may concern a branch changed by the response to it. Record the difference between what was predicted, what was done and who paid for it.

## The Signal and The Sounding

Never define the cosmic source. Helpful information can work and still be partial. Observation shifts branches; 2030 steering displaces costs. The Sounding remains the larger unknown, with no approved fixed date or universal solution. No compulsory evacuation ending.

## Existing campaign moves retained

**Say It Yourself:** At Static 3+, when you catch yourself being helpful in a way you cannot account for, name it aloud and mark XP. If the GM notices first, mark Static instead.

**The Water (GM, once per session):** Keep the existing move label for continuity; open with one concrete sentence about local conditions or a displaced cost. Examples in the fictional scenario: “The delivery is on time; the clinic has no driver.” “The corrected manifest reached the desk before its author.” Water need not rise between every scene, and this is not a claim of inevitable real-world inundation.

**Playing What You Lost:** At Static 5, replace the claimed character. When the GM brings them back, the original player can voice their offer of concrete help. Their words do not grant the player omniscience or settle a case before Theorize.

## Stakes and continuity

Keep personal obligations and human connections. Violence is available under the core moves; it does not settle what a warning meant, and its consequences include access, trust and people harmed. Do not use an invented licensing law as an automatic moral punishment.

A shared household network can connect the crew, but no common ancestry, birthplace or childhood is mandatory. Read obligations in interludes; disclose any mechanical cost before a decision. Keep known records intact even when branches conflict.

## Session checklist

- Choose the scenario date and which timeline events are already known.
- Establish actual places, fictional premises and a job.
- Let each player define their connection to the crew and a specific person they owe.
- Freely choose playbooks; preserve existing move/stat rules.
- Present one discrepancy, not the explanation.
- Track who knew what before each intervention.
- Build later cases around the group's established findings.
